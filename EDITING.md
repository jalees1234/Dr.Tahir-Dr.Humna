# Personalising the invitation
All invitation content is in public/wedding-config.json. Change the names, song, backgroundPhoto, and each event's time, venue, video, and poster there. Put replacement files in public/media and use paths such as /media/my-song.mp3. Upload your own licensed song and wedding clips; MP4 H.264 video and MP3 audio work well on phones. Use short, compressed landscape clips. The original instrumental melody and animated venue-photo clips are starter media, not footage of the couple. The general background uses a stock celebration photograph until a couple photo is provided.

Dates currently assume Mehndi 18, Barat 19, Walima 20 December 2026. The countdown starts celebrations at midnight Pakistan time on December 18; edit countdownTo with +05:00 once the actual start time is known. Venue and time are deliberately marked To be announced.

After changing files, rebuild and redeploy. This works for all guests; files are not kept only in your browser. There is no RSVP.
